Data science is the discipline of turning raw, noisy observations into structured understanding and reliable decisions. Unlike traditional software engineering—where rules are explicitly programmed—data science infers rules directly from empirical evidence.

1.1 The Data Science Lifecycle

Every real-world analytical system moves through iterative stages: problem formulation, data acquisition, cleaning, exploratory analysis, modeling, and deployment.

Key Principle A simple statistical model trained on clean, well-understood features consistently outperforms a complex deep-learning model trained on unverified data.

1.2 Mathematical Notation
